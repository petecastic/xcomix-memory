How to play

  • Raiders walk in from the right along 5 lanes. Don't let them reach the left edge.
  • Tap a card at the top, then tap a tile to place that defender. Each card costs energy and needs to recharge.
  • Tap the glowing drops to collect energy: Vending Crumbs in the museum, Shiny Shells on the pier, Sakura Rice Stars at the Mart. Your producers (Crumbs, Lil Sook, Tamago-chan) make more. Turn on AUTO if tapping is hard on your phone.
  • DIG removes a defender and gives back a quarter of its cost.
  • Each lane has one last-ditch sweeper on the left: the Museum Floor Buffer, the Harbor Wave Barrel and the Rolling Bento Cart. They clear the lane once.
  • Watch the flag meter. "A HUGE WAVE IS APPROACHING!" means a big push is coming.
  • Clear stages to unlock new defenders, then the BOSS stage, then ENDLESS.
  • Keys: 1-9 pick a card, S digs, P or Space pauses, Esc cancels.

Timeline notes

  • Another timeline, all in fun. Nobody gets hurt: raiders get shooed, swept, stuck or sent home.
  • The Old Bay Boys are live blue crabs. Always. The harbor wheel is a made-up googly-eyed wheel.
  • No real brands. Every snack, can and sign is made up.
