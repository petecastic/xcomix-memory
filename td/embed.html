How to play

  • Pick a tower in the bar, then tap a free tile. On a phone you tap once to preview and again to place. With a mouse, hold Shift to keep placing.
  • Tap a placed tower to upgrade it. Each tower has two paths. Only one can reach tier 3.
  • Hit START to send wave 1. You can call the next wave early once the current one is fully out. You get the wave bonus when you call it.
  • Some enemies are hidden and look faded. They need a tower that can see them: Salt, Lumos, Lt. Rumple, or the right upgrade.
  • Flying enemies (gulls, puffs, Echo moths) ignore Krampus, Big Jimmy, Stanley and the Harbor Wheel.
  • Lives: an enemy that reaches the crack costs 1. A boss costs 20. You get 3 stars for keeping 90% of your lives and 2 stars for 50%.
  • XP from every game levels you up and unlocks new towers. Clearing a map opens the next one, plus its Hard and Endless modes.
  • The daily challenge gives everyone the same map, the same rule twist and 5 borrowed towers.
  • Watch for Zib. Tap him for a little help.
  • Keys: 1-9 pick a tower, Space sends the next wave, P pauses, Esc cancels.

Timeline notes

  • Another timeline. None of this is canon. A 12:49 crack has folded Bristol, Philly and the Pike onto Mars. Nobody gets hurt: enemies are shooed, soaked, calmed, ticketed or sent home.
  • Domino & Pickle sit this one out. Their show doesn't do crossovers, so they're not here.
  • Echoes of the Red Horizon is Season 1 only. Salt, Lumos, Zib, Tank Morrow, the Overmind and the Mars hazards (Cinder Mites, Echo Moths, Phobos Puffs, Dustrunners, Hollowhounds, the Regolith Crawler) all come from Season 1. Later threats are left out.
  • Mars has no rowhomes. The Earth houses you see on Mars are blue Echo projections, not real buildings.
  • Pete is a cable tech. Krampus works the poles. Pete runs the coax.
  • The geese here are generic geese. Bucky is benched.
  • No real brands. Every snack, shop and sign is made up.
